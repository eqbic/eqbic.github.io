## about
Hey, I'm Erich — a software developer based in Berlin with a background in media informatics and a decade of experience building interactive systems at the intersection of software and the physical world.

I work on real-time systems, object and touch recognition, and interactive 3D applications. My day-to-day involves writing Rust and Python at [Interactive Scape](https://www.interactive-scape.com/), where I develop the software stack for multitouch table hardware — from sensor data processing and AI-based recognition pipelines to customer-facing tools and open source projects.

Before that, I spent several years at the digital agency [intolight](https://www.intolight.de/) in Dresden, building interactive exhibits for museums, trade fairs, and events: touch interfaces, projection mapping, VR experiences, and game-like installations. That work taught me to move fast across unfamiliar domains while delivering reliable, polished results for real audiences.

I am always interested in broadening my horizons and immersing myself in new topics. Whether it's diving into a new programming language, a different problem domain, or an unfamiliar hardware platform — figuring things out is what I enjoy most about this work.
